# JJW Window & Door Installations, INC. — Landing Page

Official responsive landing page for **JJW Window & Door Installations, INC.** based in Deltona, FL, serving Volusia, Seminole, Orange, Brevard, Flagler, and Marion counties.

## Highlights & Specifications

- **Reference Layout Fidelity:** Recreated the exact layout, section rhythm, color tokens, and visual structure from the reference screenshots (Top utility bar, arched emblem logo, hero slider with centered white typography, 2-column intro with BBB A+ accreditation and 5-star customer review pill, 3-tier interactive service accordions, dual full-width parallax viewports, 3-column feature grid with coral checkmark cards, real Central Florida field work showcase, and soft ice-blue footer with payment badges).
- **Parallax Scroll:** Full-width hero and banner sections feature smooth parallax scrolling (`parallax-bg`).
- **Interactive Modals:**
  - **Free Estimate Calculator Modal:** Supports interactive inquiry submissions, service selection, and confetti confirmation.
  - **Service Detail Modal:** Triggered via "View More" on services, providing full Florida building code, masonry block prep, and Low-E glass specs.
  - **Project Lightbox Modal:** Displays high-resolution installation photography with before & after scope details.
- **Brand Standards & Guardrails:**
  - Zero all-caps text anywhere (all Sentence Case and Title Case).
  - Zero glowing or pulsing dots.
  - Authentic, un-tinted photographic backgrounds.
  - Humanized, direct copy adhering to `no-ai-slop` & `stop-slop`.
- **Responsive Across All Breakpoints:** Tested and verified on desktop (1440px+), laptop, tablet (768px), and mobile (375px).
